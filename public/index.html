🛡️ Privacy & Terms Agreement

×

Before creating an account on Sudoku Arena, please review our data privacy commitment:

1. Data We Store

We store only your chosen username, match statistics, scores, and approved friendships. We do not collect real names, emails, phone numbers, or location tracking data.

2. Cryptographic Password Protection

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Every password is salt-hashed using bcryptjs (10 rounds) before saving to our database.

3. Friend Presence

Your online presence is shared strictly with confirmed friends on your list via real-time WebSocket sessions to facilitate games.

4. How to Delete Your Data

You have full ownership of your data. You can delete your account and all associated statistics at any time through:

  • Self-Service: Tap 📊 Profile → click "🗑️ Permanently Delete My Account & Data".
  • Admin Support: Contact the server admin to remove your records directly from the Admin Portal.
